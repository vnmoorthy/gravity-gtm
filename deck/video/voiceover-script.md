# Gravity: voiceover script (~2:15, speak it, don't read it)

**How to record:** one take, phone voice memo or QuickTime, in a quiet room. Leave **~1 second of silence between sections**; I auto-align each section to its shot. Talk to one person, not a room. Smile on the bold words. If you stumble, pause and redo that section. Save as `deck/video/voiceover.m4a`.

---

**1 · Open** *(title card)*
Hi, I'm [your name].
Every founder knows the outbound grind: five tools, generic AI emails, data you can't trust.
So we built **Gravity**, a go-to-market engine that *shows its work*.

**2 · Intake**
It starts with **one sentence**.
I tell Gravity what we sell, add my team's network, and hit **Launch**.

**3 · War room**
Now eleven agents get to work, out loud.
A planner hands off to a scout that's searching the live web with Claude.
A **RocketRide** pipeline cleans every signal, and **Flexprice** meters every step.

**4 · ICP**
First, it figures out who actually buys: industries, personas, trigger events.
And it's honest about it. Every field has a **confidence score**.
It even suggests new segments, and *I* decide.

**5 · Accounts**
These are **real companies**. Every claim links to a source.
If it can't cite it, it **drops it**.
And every contact is verified: DNS, mail servers, then Hunter.

**6 · Ask**
I can just *ask* it. "CFOs hiring after funding."
**Moss** answers in milliseconds.

**7 · Graph**
Underneath, it's a **knowledge graph**.
It finds the warm way in, like who on my team knows someone there,
and which accounts are ready to switch from a competitor.

**8 · Review**
Then it writes: short, specific, two variants.
Here's my favorite part. Watch me slip in a **lie**.
The reviewer catches it, strikes it, and the predicted reply odds collapse.

**9 · Approve**
Nothing goes out without me. **One click** to approve.

**10 · Pipeline**
Every lead flows through the pipeline,
and I always know what the **AI** did versus what a **human** did.

**11 · Analytics**
Analytics shows what's working: by tier, by variant, down to **cost per meeting**.

**12 · Billing**
And it's built as a business. Every action is a usage event in **Flexprice**.
Run out of credits, and it **stops**, until you top up.

**13 · Data**
All of it lands in **HotData**, so the team can query it in SQL.
And through our MCP server, **Teamily** agents approve outreach right from the group chat.

**14 · Close** *(face-cam if you can)*
That's Gravity. **One sentence in, booked meetings out.**
Self-driving, but *you* hold the brakes, and the wallet.
